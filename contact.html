Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! All our services — vet consultations, grooming, and training — can be done at your home across Karachi.

For most services, same-day or next-day appointments are available. Emergency vet visits can often be arranged within hours.

We accept cash on delivery, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and bank transfer for all services.
